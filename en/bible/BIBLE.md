# Style bible for [CHANNEL NAME]

Version 1.0. The same rules as `style.yaml`, with their reasons. If this file and the YAML disagree, the YAML wins, because it's what runs. That's why they always change together.

Every rule follows the same format: the rule, why it exists, one yes example and one no example.

## 1. Composition and text zone

### The lower third stays free for text
Rule: the bottom 30% of the frame has low contrast and no busy texture.
Why: that's where the lyrics or on-screen text go. A busy background makes them unreadable.
Yes: references/approved/yes_meadow-dawn_v1.png
No: references/rejected/no_unreadable-lyrics_1104.png

## 2. Palette and light

### Golden side light
Rule: light comes in from the side, with gold `#D9A55B` dominant.
Why: it's the channel's signature and what sets it apart in the recommendations grid.
Yes: references/approved/yes_coastal-path_v1.png
No: references/rejected/no_neon-off-palette_1102.png

## 3. Characters

### Characters from behind or at a distance
Rule: no face takes more than 10% of the frame.
Why: faces drift between pieces and cause most rejections.
Yes: references/approved/yes_meadow-walkers_v1.png
No: references/rejected/no_close-up-face_1102.png
