# Approved pieces

Store the images or frames the owner approved here. They define the style better than any text.

- Name: `yes_<scene>_v<version>.png`, for example `yes_meadow-dawn_v1.png`. A descriptive name lets BIBLE.md cite it and lets the agent know what it shows without opening it.
- Count: 10 to 30 to start. With 20 to 30 you can train your own style model (a LoRA or custom model).
- Only pieces the owner would approve today without changes.
